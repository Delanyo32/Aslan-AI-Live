# Demo Backtest Queries

A collection of queries to test the NewsTrader AI pipeline.

---

## Geopolitical / Trade

- Buy Nvidia every time the US announces new AI chip export restrictions on China
- Short semiconductor stocks whenever the US adds Chinese tech companies to the entity list
- Buy defense stocks every time North Korea tests a ballistic missile
- Short Apple every time US-China trade tensions escalate significantly

## Federal Reserve / Macro

- Short bank stocks every time the Fed raises interest rates unexpectedly
- Buy gold miners every time the Fed signals a pivot to rate cuts
- Short regional banks every time there is a US banking sector stress event

## Earnings

- Short Tesla after an earnings miss on revenue or margins
- Buy Nvidia the day after it reports better-than-expected data center revenue
- Short Meta every time it reports a decline in daily active users

## Regulatory / Legal

- Buy pharmaceutical stocks every time the FDA grants a breakthrough therapy designation
- Short Boeing every time there is a new safety incident or regulatory grounding
- Buy crypto-adjacent stocks every time the SEC approves a Bitcoin ETF or dismisses a major enforcement action

## Energy / Commodities

- Buy oil majors every time OPEC announces a surprise production cut
- Short airline stocks every time crude oil spikes more than 10% in a month

## Single-Stock Catalysts

- Buy Palantir every time the US government announces a major AI or defense contract
- Short Disney every time it reports a streaming subscriber miss
- Buy AMD every time Intel announces a product delay or fab setback
